Privacy policy

What CassaCheck keeps when you check a leaf, who can see it, and when it is deleted.

Version of 18 November 2026

inventory_2

What we collect

  • When you check a leaf, we keep the time of the check, the result (a condition or "not sure"), how confident the model was, and which version of the model gave it.
  • If you choose a district and a season, we keep them with the result.
  • If you answer the questions after a "not sure" result, we keep your answers. Only the choices on the buttons are kept. You cannot type your own text.
  • If you send the case to an extension officer, we keep a case number, a check code, the case status and the officer's note.
  • We keep a phone number only if you tick the consent box and type it.
block

What we never collect

  • Your photo is not stored. The server looks at it to give the result and then drops it.
  • If the photo does not look like a leaf, it is refused and nothing is kept.
  • We do not ask for your name. There is no account and no password for farmers.
  • We do not read your phone's location.
  • We do not use cookies or advertising trackers.
help

Why we keep it

  • So the extension officer can see which results come from which districts and seasons.
  • So the officer can follow up a case you sent, and you can read the answer on "My case".
  • Your phone number is used only so the officer can call you about that one case.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
visibility

Who can see it

  • You see your result on your phone. On "My case" you see your case status and the officer's note, but only with the case number and the check code.
  • Extension officers see the list of checks, the answers and the cases sent to them. The list is locked with an access code.
  • The person who runs CassaCheck can open the database to keep the app working.
  • The app runs on servers of hosting companies. Render, which hosts the app, may record your internet address (IP address) and the pages you open, as most websites do.
  • The pages load their fonts and styles from Google Fonts and Tailwind CSS. Those services receive your internet address when a page opens.
call

How we use your phone number

  • The phone box appears only after you tick "I agree that the extension officer may call me about this case." The server refuses a number sent without that tick.
  • Only extension officers see it, behind the access code. It is never shown on the farmer pages, not even on "My case".
  • When an officer downloads the list as a file, phone numbers are left out, because a downloaded file cannot be deleted later.
  • It is deleted when the officer marks your case answered, or after 30 days, whichever comes first.
schedule

How long we keep it

  • Phone number: until the case is answered, or 30 days at most (see the limits below).
  • The result, your answers, the case status and the officer's note: there is no automatic deletion date yet.
  • On your phone, the last result and a small copy of your photo stay in the browser tab until you close it.
  • On your phone, "My case" remembers up to 10 cases you sent, with their case number and check code, until you remove them. Your language choice is also remembered.
tune

Your choices

  • You can use CassaCheck without giving a phone number. The officer can still see your case, and you can show your case number at the sector office.
  • You can check a leaf without choosing a district or season.
  • You decide whether to answer the questions and whether to send the case to an officer.
  • You can remove a remembered case from "My case" on your phone, and close the tab to clear the last result.
  • If you change your mind about sharing your number, write to the contact below. Your number is deleted when your case is marked answered.
shield

How we protect it

  • The officer list is checked on the server, not only hidden on the page. Without the right access code, it stays closed. If no code is set on the server, it is closed to everyone.
  • The access code is kept in the hosting settings, never written in the app's code.
  • Each case has a random six-character check code. A wrong code and a case that does not exist give the same answer, so cases cannot be guessed one by one.
  • Your check code is sent inside the request, never in the web address, so it does not appear in your browser history or in the list of web addresses the host records.
  • The live site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
report

Limits we want you to know

  • The 30-day deletion of phone numbers runs when an officer opens the list, not on a timer. If nobody opens the list, a number can stay longer than 30 days.
  • All extension officers share one access code. Anyone who has it can see the list, including phone numbers. Separate accounts for each officer are the next step.
  • Once an officer has called you, your number may stay in their phone's call history or contacts. CassaCheck deletes it from the app, but it cannot delete it from the officer's own phone.
  • The phone number is not encrypted inside the database. It is protected by the access code and by the hosting company.
  • A result is a help for your decision, not a confirmed diagnosis. When the model is not sure, it says "not sure" and lets you send the case to an extension officer.
  • CassaCheck never recommends a pesticide or a dose. The officer's note cannot name one either.
